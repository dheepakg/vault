title: My version of Second brain
---



My 2<sup>nd</sup> brain holds the notes from different areas. The primary purpose is not only archiving the notes but to retrieve via the search. The vault is organised into,
1. Books - [Notes & highlights](./Books) from books I read
2. Quotes - Some [interesting sayings](./Quotes) from achievers, I try to have some context on the person who said it.
3. Studies - These are the notes from [my learnings](./Studies)
4. TIL - [Some intriguing](./TIL) things that I come across. Could be some trivia or weird facts.
