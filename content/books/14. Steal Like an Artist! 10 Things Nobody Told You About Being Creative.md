It’s one of my theories that when people give you advice, they’re really just talking to themselves in the past. (Location 28)

  

**Note:** Advise

  

André Gide put it, “Everything that needs to be said has already been said. But, since no one was listening, everything must be said again.” (Location 54)

  

**Note:** Nothing is new

  

You are, in fact, a mashup of what you choose to let into your life. You are the sum of your influences. The German writer Goethe said, “We are shaped and fashioned by what we love.” (Location 69)

  

**Note:** Mashup

  

Your job is to collect good ideas. The more good ideas you collect, the more you can choose from to be influenced by. (Location 81)

  

**Note:** Ideas in

  

chew on one thinker—writer, artist, activist, role model—you really love. Study everything there is to know about that thinker. Then find three people that thinker loved, and find out everything about them. Repeat this as many times as you can. (Location 89)

  

**Note:** Learn from history

  

Carry a notebook and a pen with you wherever you go. Get used to pulling it out and jotting down your thoughts and observations. (Location 109)

  

**Note:** Get more ideas

  

We don’t know where we get our ideas from. What we do know is that we do not get them from our laptops.” —John Cleese (Location 234)

  

**Note:** Ideas

  

“The work you do while you procrastinate is probably the work you should be doing for the rest of your life.” —Jessica Hische (Location 282)

  

**Note:** Procrastinate

  

If you have two or three real passions, don’t feel like you have to pick and choose between them. Don’t discard. Keep all your passions in your life. This is something I learned from the playwright Steven Tomlinson. (Location 296)

  

**Note:** Multiple passion

  

You can’t connect the dots looking forward, you can only connect them looking backwards.” —Steve Jobs (Location 299)

  

**Note:** Connecting dots

  

Tomlinson suggests that if you love different things, you just keep spending time with them. “Let them talk to each other. Something will begin to happen.” (Location 302)

  

**Note:** Cross polination

  

Franz Kafka wrote, “It isn’t necessary that you leave home. Sit at your desk and listen. Don’t even listen, just wait. Don’t wait, be still and alone. The whole world will offer itself to you. (Location 379)

  

**Note:** World comes to you

  

“Distance and difference are the secret tonic of creativity. When we get home, home is still the same. But something in our mind has been changed, and that changes everything.” —Jonah Lehrer (Location 391)

  

**Note:** Leave home

  

“Complain about the way other people make software by making software.” —Andre Torrez (Location 438)

  

**Note:** Fix your problems

  

Hugh MacLeod says, “The best way to get approval is to not need it.” (Location 444)

  

**Note:** Approval

  

“Be regular and orderly in your life, so that you may be violent and original in your work.” —Gustave Flaubert (Location 472)

  

**Note:** Be boring

  

It takes a lot of energy to be creative. You don’t have that energy if you waste it on other stuff. (Location 477)

  

**Note:** Preserve energy

  

Eat breakfast. Do some push-ups. Go for long walks. Get plenty of sleep. (Location 479)

  

Neil Young sang, “It’s better to burn out than to fade away.” (Location 480)
