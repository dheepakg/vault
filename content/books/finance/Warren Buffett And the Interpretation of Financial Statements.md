## Introduction

> [!quote] Quotes
> "You have to understand accounting and you have to understand the nuances of accounting. It's the language of business and it's an imperfect language, but unless you are willing to put in the effort to learn accounting - how to read and interpret financial statements - you really shouldn't select stocks yourself."
> — Warren Buffett




Warren has figured out that the super companies come in 2 basic business models
 1. They sell unique product or services
 2. they are low cost buyer & seller of a product or service that public consistently needs.

**Income Statement** - How much money a company earned during a period of time.
**Balance Sheet** - Its a snapshot on that particular day. Tells us how much money the company has in the bank and how much money it owes.
**Cashflow Statement** - Tracks how much money flows in and pout of the company.
	
 
## The Income Statement

To Warren, the source of the earnings is always more important than the earnings themselves.

1. **Cost of Goods Sold**
	- Cost of purchasing the goods the company is reselling or cost of materials and labour used in manufacturing the products its selling
	- Aka Cost of revenue if it deals with services
2. **Gross Profit Margin**

 
  %% $$Gross Profit Margin = {Gross Profit \over Total revenue}$$     %%
	- Greater than 40% → Has durable competitive advantages
	- Less than 20% → Company is part of fiercely competitive industry
	
3. **Selling, General & Administrative Expenses**
	- Companies don't have a durable competitive advantage suffer from intense competition and show wild variation in SGA costs as percentage of gross profit
	- If company can't cut SGA costs fast enough, they start eating into more and more of the company's gross profits.
	- SGA > 100% → company might be part of highly competitive industry
4. **Research & Development**
	- If competitive advantage is the result of some technological advancements, there is always the threat that newer technology will replace it
	- Warren's rule: Companies that have to spend heavily on R&D have an inherent flaw in their competitive advantage that will always put their long-term economics at risk.
5. **Depreciation**
	- Warren believes that depreciation is a very real expense and should always be included in any calculation of earnings.
	- Less means good
6. **Interest Expense**
	- In an industry, the company with lowest ratio of interest payments to operating income is usually the company most likely to have the competitive advantage
7. **Gain (Loss) on Sale of Assets**
	- This shouldn't be considered in the calculation of the earnings.
8. **Net Earnings**
	- A single year's earning is worthless of Warren, he looks for historical upward trend to measure durability
	- Net Earnings > 20% of total revenue → Long-term competitive advantage
	- Net Earnings between 10% and 20% of total revenue → Could be a gold thats not discovered
	- Net Earnings < 10% of total revenue → Could be part competitive industry
	- **Warnings** - Bank/Financial companies with high ratio of net earnings to revenue usually means its slacking off in risk management department. 
		- Recipe for diaster
9. **Per Share Earnings**
	- Look for 10 year period of consistency & up trend
	- Consistent earnings is sign of the company is selling a product that don't need to go through the expensive process of change
