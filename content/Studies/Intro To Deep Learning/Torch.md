 As I understand, [torch](https://github.com/torch/torch7) is an open-source language used for machine learning. The initial development of torch is in 2002, during the phase when machine learning was limited to academia and its not _cool_ thing to do. Torch was built using Lua and C. This is the second time I'm coming across Lua, while looking up for [[HammerSpoon]].


As of now, torch is no longer actively developed. Torch development moved in 2017 to PyTorch, a port of the library to Python. 

According to popular opinion, Pytorch is better than Tensorflow. Earlier I came across [[fast.ai]] picking [pytorch over Tensorflow & Keras](https://www.fast.ai/posts/2017-09-08-introducing-pytorch-for-fastai.html#some-pytorch-benefits-for-us-and-our-students).
